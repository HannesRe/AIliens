🎯 Our Mission

The "Real or Fake?" AI Challenge aims to educate and engage people in understanding artificial intelligence-generated images. We believe that as AI technology continues to advance, it's crucial for everyone to develop the skills to identify AI-generated content. Through this interactive quiz, we help people learn to spot the subtle differences between real photographs and AI-created images.

🔬 Why This Matters

With the rapid advancement of AI technology, deepfakes and AI-generated images are becoming increasingly realistic. Our research helps us understand how humans perceive and interact with AI-generated content. Your participation contributes to valuable insights about human perception and AI literacy in the digital age.

📊 Our Research

This project is part of ongoing research into human-AI interaction and the effectiveness of AI detection methods. All data is collected with your consent and handled according to strict privacy standards. We analyze patterns in how people identify AI images before and after receiving guidance on detection techniques.
