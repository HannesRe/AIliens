HOW TOPLAY

1

Start the Quiz

Before you can begin, we need your approximate age and a few other details. *

2

Analyze the Image

You will be shown pictures of people. Decide whether you think they are real photographs of people or whether they are AI-generated. Select the appropriate button.Please also tell us how sure you are by using the slider.

3

Make Your Choice

You will then receive a short tutorial on how to better recognize Ki images.

4

Confidence Level

Please use your newly acquired knowledge to reassess whether the portraits are AI-generated.

📋 What to Expect

This game is part of a survey conducted by the SES laboratory at OTH Regensburg. With this game, we want to measure how well AI-generated images can be distinguished from real ones. In order to differentiate these statistics by age, we need to know your age. If you would like to know more about this project, click here.
